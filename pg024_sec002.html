Eyes

The eyes are used to see and identify things. They are located on the left and right of the front side of the face. The eyes are held in position by the muscles. These muscles rotate and move the eye side to side, up and down.

Parts of the eyes

The human eye is made up of two major parts, which are the outer and inner parts. The outer part consists of eyebrows, eyelashes and eyelids. Figure 3 shows the outer parts of the human eye.

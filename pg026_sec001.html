Parts of the eye and their functions

Parts of the eye perform various functions to enable proper vision. The parts of the eye and their functions are explained in Table 1.

Table 1: Parts of the eye and their functions

Main parts Parts Function
External part Eyebrows Prevent sweat, dust and other objects from entering the eye.
Eyelids Cover the eye to prevent objects from entering it.
Eyelashes Prevent dust from entering the eye.
Internal part Pupil Allows light to enter the eye
Iris Regulates the amount of light that enters the eye.
Retina Contains cells that are sensitive to light and enables the image to be formed.
Lens Focus light into the retina.

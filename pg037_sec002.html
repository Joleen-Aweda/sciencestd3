The importance of sense organs

Sense organs are very important in the human body. Sense organs help us to detect or recognise changes in our environment and respond accordingly. Any damage to our sense organs affects the ability to receive or detect the stimuli from the environment. For example, defects in the ears can cause deafness and defects in the eyes can cause blindness.

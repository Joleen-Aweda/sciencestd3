Vocabulary

Organ

part of the body which performs a specific function. Example of organs are ears, nose, tongue, eyes and skin

Taste buds

special cells in the tongue with nerves that collect information about the taste of food

Umami

a combination of tastes which are found in foods such as meat, milk and fish

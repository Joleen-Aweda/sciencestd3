General characteristics of birds

  1. 1.
    They have a streamlined body, which helps them to fly easily.
  2. 2.
    Their bodies are covered with feathers, which help them to maintain body temperature.
  3. 3.
    They have wings, which help them to fly.
  4. 4.
    They have tough pointed beaks, without teeth.
  5. 5.
    They reproduce by laying eggs.
  6. 6.
    They have two legs which are covered with scutes.
  7. 7.
    They are warm-blooded. That means their body temperature does not change with the environment.

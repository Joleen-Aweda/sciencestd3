Procedure

  • 1. Take a maize plant or any other plant and place it on a table.
  • 2. Observe the plant and identify its parts.

Questions

  • (a) In which part of a plant are leaves borne?
  • (b) In which part of a plant are roots found?
  • (c) In which part of a plant is the stem found?

Parts of a plant

Steps in conducting a scientific investigation

A scientific investigation is conducted by following a systematic procedure. It involves problem identification, hypothesis formulation, preparing materials for experiment, conducting an experiment and collecting data, analysing data, interpreting results, conclusion and writing a scientific report.

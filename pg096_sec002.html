Activity 5
Using logic with GCompris or Starfall

Name of the game: Simplified Tower of Hanoi in GCompris or a patterns activity in Starfall

Goal: To use logic, patterns and order to complete a task

Duration: 20 minutes

Requirements: Desktop computer, laptop, tablet or mobile phone with the game installed

Instructions

1.

Study the tower on the right and its description, including the signs on each piece shown in Figure 5. Your goal is to build an identical tower in an empty area by moving one piece at a time.

2.

Move only the top piece of a tower. You can drag and drop it, or select it with Enter, use the arrow keys to choose another tower or the empty area, and press Enter again to place it. Refer to Figure 5 and its description.

Construct the tower in the empty space similar to the one on the right.

Figure 5: Simplified Tower of Hanoi game

Note: You can attempt to complete different levels of the game and master more challenging stages.
